title: Inviting Team Members
description: Outcome Partners: Add your team to COSSA.
---

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Organization Settings">
    Log in and click your profile, then select "Organization Settings".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Team Members">
    Click the "Team Members" tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send Invitation">
    Click "Invite Member" and enter their email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Permissions">
    Choose a role (e.g., Viewer, Editor) and send invite.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
Invited users will receive a sign-up link by email.
</Callout>
