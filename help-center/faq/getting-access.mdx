---
title: How do Outcome Partners get access to COSSA?
description: Steps to join COSSA as an Outcome Partner.
---

## Overview

Outcome Partners can request an account through their organization or directly from COSSA support.

## Steps

1. Go to `https://cossa.org/signup`
2. Choose "Outcome Partner" as your role
3. Complete the registration form
4. Confirm your email address
5. Wait for admin approval (1-2 days)

<Callout kind="info">
Already have an account? Log in at [`https://cossa.org/login`](https://cossa.org/login).
</Callout>
