## Overview

COSSA unites founders and investors to create thriving commercial open source businesses. You gain access to a collaborative platform that fosters connections, shares resources, and drives sustainable growth in the open source ecosystem. Join thousands of builders turning innovative projects into enduring companies.

## Quick Start

Get up and running in minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up" icon="user-plus">
    Visit [cossa.io](https://cossa.io) and create your free account using your email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Profile" icon="edit-3">
    Add your role (founder or investor), project details, and funding stage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join a Room" icon="message-circle">

  </Step>
